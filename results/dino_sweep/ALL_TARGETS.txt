    perturbed                        24       2     0.50        1.00  CONFOUNDED: plate determines this label
    balance      = fraction in the largest class (chance for a
                   majority-class guesser)
    plate->label = accuracy of predicting the label from plate
                   ALONE. Near 1.0 means the label IS the plate:
                   any batch-encoding feature 'predicts' it, and
                   --deconfound plate will remove the whole signal
                   because there is nothing else there.

  staged 'gfp' volumes on disk: 72

  how to read these
    eta2   raw between-plate share of variance. BIASED UPWARD: with
           k plates and n replicates its expected value under NO
           plate effect is (k-1)/(n-1), shown as 'null'. Never
           compare eta2 to zero.
    omega2 eta2 corrected for that bias. ~0 means the label has no
           more plate structure than chance -> testable. Large means
           the label is largely a plate property, so any batch-
           encoding feature predicts it and holding plates out
           leaves too few independent units.
